Overlap — Russian (ru) glossary
Address: polite «вы», lowercase. Quotes «…» (inner „…“). Buttons: infinitive («Сохранить»).
Gender-neutral wording for the user: «вы» + plural past tense; avoid сам(а).
Plurals: one / few / many / other (other = few form, for fractions).
Ranges: times «9:00–17:00» (en dash, no spaces); dates «{from} — {to}».
