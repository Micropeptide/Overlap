Turkish (tr) glossary for Overlap. Polite "siz" in sentences; buttons use the short bare imperative (Kaydet, Kopyala) as is usual in Turkish UIs.
No suffix is ever attached to a {placeholder}: use "“{title}” anketi", "{name} adlı kişi", "{date} günü/tarihinde", "{zone} saati", "{count} kişi".
