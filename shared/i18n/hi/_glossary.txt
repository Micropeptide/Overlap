Hindi (hi) glossary for Overlap. Polite "आप", imperative -एँ forms (करें, चुनें).
Sentence end: । (purna viram); ? and ! as usual. Quotes: “…”. Nukta on ज़, फ़ (ज़रूरत, सिर्फ़, हफ़्ता, फ़ाइल).
Plural categories: one (n = 0 or 1), other. Every "one" form keeps {count}, since 0 uses it too.
